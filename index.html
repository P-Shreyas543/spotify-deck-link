Spotify Deck

An ESP32 touchscreen that shows what Spotify is playing, with album art, play/pause, skip and volume. Open source, set up in two minutes with your phone.

Install on your board Source code on GitHub

Features

Now playing

Album art, scrolling title, artists, album and a live progress bar. Podcasts too.

Touch controls

Previous, play/pause, next and volume with instant feedback (Spotify Premium).

Two-minute setup

Scan a QR code for Wi-Fi, scan another to log in to Spotify. No app, no laptop.

Updates over Wi-Fi

Install new firmware from a browser; it rolls back by itself if anything goes wrong.

Every artist name

Accented and Cyrillic names render properly: Beyoncé, Måneskin, Dvořák, Кино.

Open and tested

MIT licensed ESP-IDF + LVGL firmware with on-device unit tests and hardware-in-the-loop tests.

What you need

  • Elecrow CrowPanel 2.4" ESP32 HMI (also sold as the "ESP32 Miner LCD 2.4", SKU DHM04624D): ESP32, 320x240 touchscreen, USB-C. No soldering.
  • A USB-C cable and a computer with Chrome or Edge (for the one-time install).
  • A phone, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and a Spotify account (Premium for the controls).

Install

  1. Connect the board to this computer with USB.
  2. Click the button and choose the board's serial port (CH340 / USB-SERIAL).
Your browser cannot reach USB devices: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer needs an https page (open this site from github.io).

The firmware on this page signs in to Spotify with the maintainer's developer app, which Spotify limits to 5 accounts. If linking says your account is not allowed, build with your own free Spotify app (5 minutes): build from source. Installing erases the board.

Set it up

  1. Welcome: tap Start.
  2. Wi-Fi: scan the QR code with your phone camera; pick your network on the page that opens.
  3. Spotify: with the phone back on your Wi-Fi, scan the next QR code, log in and tap Agree.
  4. Play music on any device: the deck shows it.

Later: the gear button opens Settings (Wi-Fi, Spotify, touch calibration, firmware update, factory reset). Updates: http://spotifydeck.local/update.

How the Spotify login works

Spotify only returns to https:// addresses after login, and the deck is a plain-HTTP device on your home network. So the login (OAuth with PKCE) is started by the deck, Spotify sends your phone back to this page, and this page immediately forwards it to the deck, which finishes the login. Nothing is stored here, and the one-time code is useless without a secret that never leaves the deck. Privacy.
